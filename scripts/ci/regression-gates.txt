# Row format: <test file>|<gate|pin>|<declaration>
#   struct SuiteName          a whole-suite gate, or a pin
#   func SuiteName.methodName a method gate, qualified by its suite

# are not gates. Text from "#" to the end of a line is a comment.
#
# Every gate and pin must also appear in the gate lists of
# docs/reference/architecture-conventions.md, AGENTS.md and CONTRIBUTING.md.
